// A look at the night watch, over the pit: out of range from here.
look 10.0 4.0 23.5
wait 1.5
// One: the bridge into the dividing wall, and out beside the guard post,
// whose shadow hides the cube from the watch.
shoot orange 13.99 2.6 7.5
shoot blue 5.5 3.0 1.0
walk_to 5.5 3.0
walk_to 5.5 10.4
grab
wait 0.4
// Back, and the cube onto its button: door a opens.
walk_to 5.5 4.0
walk_to 11.5 4.8
look 11.5 2.3 3.5
wait 0.4
use
wait 0.5
// Two: the bridge up the duct, behind the wall.
walk_to 16.5 3.0
shoot blue 16.5 3.0 1.0
walk_to 16.5 5.0
walk_to 16.5 25.5
// Three: from the landing, the bridge across the moat, behind the watch.
// Relieve one of them, and put it on the exit's button, facing the wall.
shoot blue 18.99 3.0 25.5
walk_to 8.5 25.5
grab
wait 0.4
walk_to 2.6 26.4
look 1.5 2.3 26.5
wait 0.4
use
wait 0.6
walk_to 2.5 28.5
