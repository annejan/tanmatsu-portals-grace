solution
// The cube hangs at the end of the funnel, in the beam, facing north:
// the light goes on past the catcher.
look 14.7 3.5 8.5
wait 1.0
// One: the laser into the white patch on the barrier and out of the west
// wall, through relay 2: the funnel turns round and brings the cube back.
shoot orange 10.0 4.9 2.5
shoot blue 1.0 4.0 5.5
walk_to 2.6 7.6
wait 4.0
grab
wait 0.4
walk_to 6.0 7.6
// Two: the beam out of the south wall instead. It lights relay 1 alone,
// and the funnel runs forward again.
shoot blue 7.5 3.9 1.0
// Three: the cube back into the funnel, facing north-west, at the catcher.
face 315 0
wait 0.6
use
wait 4.5
// Four: the door is open, and the bridge with it.
walk_to 1.5 10.5
walk_to 1.5 13.5
walk_to 8.5 13.5
