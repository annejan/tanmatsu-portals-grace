// One: the drain's floor, where the orange gel lands and does no good.
walk_to 8.5 7.5
shoot blue 11.9 1.0 7.5
// Two: into the lane, and the white strip in its ceiling at the far end.
// The gel comes out of it and paints the last metres of the lane; then
// a little further back, so the run is long enough.
walk_to 1.5 4.0
walk_to 1.5 1.5
walk_to 11.5 1.5
shoot orange 15.75 4.0 1.5
wait 1.5
shoot orange 12.75 4.0 1.5
wait 1.5
// Three: the panel at the foot of the platform, through the gap in the
// glass, and the wall at the end of the lane.
walk_to 1.5 1.5
shoot orange 6.5 1.0 9.0
shoot blue 17.0 2.0 1.5
// Four: run. Eleven metres a second into the wall is eleven metres a
// second up out of the floor, and forward is onto the platform.
face 90 0
walk_to 17.5 1.5
walk_to 6.5 11.5
