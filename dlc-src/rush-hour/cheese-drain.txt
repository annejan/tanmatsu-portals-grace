solution
// CHEESE "drain": no gel. Blue in the drain's floor, orange in the panel
// at the foot of the platform, then walk into the drain. A body sinks a
// little way into a floor portal before it goes through, and comes out of
// the other floor as far above it: floor to floor gives back more than
// the fall put in, the more so the longer a frame is. On a slow badge
// that is enough: out of the panel at 6.9 m/s, 0.5 m up, onto the
// platform. Proven at 10-15 frames a second, not at 20-35:
//   PORTALS_CHAMBERS=<dir> build/host_tas -jitter 4 10 15 rush-hour
//   (and 10 seeds of 1..30 at -jitter N 10 12)
walk_to 8.5 7.5
shoot blue 11.9 1.0 7.5
walk_to 6.5 6.0
shoot orange 6.5 1.0 9.0
walk_to 11.0 7.5
walk_to 12.5 7.5
walk_to 6.5 11.5
