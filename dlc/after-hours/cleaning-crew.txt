solution
// One: the white cupboard's floor, and the drain out in the goo. White gel
// dropped down the pipe comes up out of the drain just as fast, and paints
// the ceiling round the skylight.
walk_to 1.5 4.5
shoot blue 1.5 1.0 1.4
walk_to 6.5 5.0
shoot orange 6.5 1.0 12.6
wait 2.5
// Two: the ceiling beside the skylight takes a portal now. Blue gel drips
// out of it and splashes onto the drain.
shoot orange 7.5 6.0 12.6
walk_to 10.5 4.5
shoot blue 10.5 1.0 1.4
wait 2.5
// Three: the cleaners' way out to the drain: into the blue cupboard's
// floor, out of the ceiling, onto the island.
walk_to 10.5 1.5
wait 1.0
// Four: a running jump off blue gel, onto the ledge.
walk_to 6.5 10.5
face 0 0
walk 0.55
jump
walk_to 6.5 17.5
