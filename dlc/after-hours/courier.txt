// One: the funnel runs along the foot of the south wall into the white
// low in the east wall. Let it come out over the pillar: it picks the
// parcel up and takes it across the room, through the slot, to the back
// of the chute.
shoot blue 16.0 1.5 1.5
shoot orange 4.5 6.0 1.0
wait 8
// Two: send it out of the other panel instead. The parcel drops down the
// chute onto the button, and the door opens.
shoot orange 11.5 6.0 1.0
// Three: you go by freight: in at the east wall, out of the south one,
// over the room and through the door.
walk_to 11.5 2.8
walk_to 14.0 1.5
wait 11.5
walk_to 14.0 22.0
