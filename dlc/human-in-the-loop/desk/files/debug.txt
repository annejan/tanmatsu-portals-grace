THE DEBUGGER                                       Aperture Science IT

Some drafts are broken: GLaDOS cannot solve them herself. When GLADOS
loads one, the debugger opens instead of the chamber.

The debugger shows the chamber from above, a layer at a time (Q and E).
It allows a few changes, of a few kinds: the draft says which, and the
panel beside the map lists them, each with the key that picks it. Usually
it is a surface made portable: metal (#) made white (W). Sometimes it is
something in the way taken out.

  arrows   move              Space   make the change
  Q / E    layer down / up   Bksp    change a cell back
  P        play-test         Esc     the debugger's menu

Changing a cell back frees the change for another. When you think it is
repaired, play-test it. Reach the exit and the repair is logged.

Do not repair the central core. The central core is not broken.
