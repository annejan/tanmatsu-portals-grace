Scoring
  A known flaw, found and logged ............... 1 point
  A novel exploit: a flaw she did not know .... 2 points
  A draft solved as intended .................. 0 points
  A final draft solved as intended ............ approval
  A hint from the central core (HINT) ......... -1 point
  A draft sent back unreviewed (DEFER) ........ 0 points
                                         (she remembers)

Conduct
  Do not feed the test subjects.
  Do not name the test subjects.
  Never discuss the test subjects with the central core.
  The central core always listens. This is a feature.

Breaks
  Breaks are scheduled by your calendar. Your calendar
  is scheduled by the central core.
